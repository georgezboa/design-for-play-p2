# NIGHTFALL — Chapter Transition Scripts V02

Status: production script and storyboard lock candidate
Scope: opening→Chapter 1 handoff plus five chapter-to-chapter films, Chapter 1→2 through Chapter 5→6
Master frame: `1920 × 1200`, 8:5, 30 fps
Runtime review: `960 × 600`, H.264, AAC 48 kHz
Target duration: 20–26 seconds per transition
Generation method: five independent MiniMax H3 shots per transition, then editorial assembly

## Shared cinematic grammar

- Butch is the only human who appears continuously. Use the approved youthful, tired face; short messy dark hair; lean build; charcoal asymmetrical work jacket; cream inner layer; practical boots; two parallel marks on the left forearm.
- Mara is not shown as a full body or face in these five transitions. Her presence is carried by a physical cyan thread, a distant voice only where specified, and evidence of an action she chose.
- Cyan is a real held relationship. Amber is a relationship travelling from source to consequence. Red is system fault or coercion.
- Every cut is motivated by a material transformation visible inside the shot. Do not use generic glitch, explosive portals, floating UI montage, or title-card-only transitions.
- Original hand-painted storybook animation language: graphite/ink contour, watercolor and gouache fields, paper grain, dry-brush edges, controlled line boil. Do not imitate a named artist, film or game.
- Avoid generated readable text inside the image. Exact messages and subtitles are composited after generation.
- The Archivist acts through cropping, aligning, compressing, labelling and forcing one format. It does not attack through generic digital noise.
- Keep faces out of extreme close-up. Prefer back, profile, three-quarter distance, hands and silhouette for continuity.
- In the opening film, the train travels through the archived worlds and each world physically rebuilds the train shell in its own material language. Continuity comes from travel direction, window rhythm, track line and carriage length; do not preserve one permanent orange-and-cream exterior across every world and do not place miniature worlds inside the windows.

## CIN OPEN→01 — THE WORLDS REBUILD THE TRAIN

Narrative job: foreshadow the journey by showing one train physically remade by the material rules of Borrowed Grid, Echo City, Painted Country and Museum; let it emerge from the final world as Chapter 1's ordinary orange-and-cream Night Service carriage, then pass through its window into the real game frame without a visible scene cut.

Duration: 22 seconds. The final 1.5 seconds are an exact game-render plate, not an AI approximation.

Authoritative handoff image: `CH01_GAMEPLAY_FIRST_FRAME_REFERENCE_V01.png`, 960×600. The locked composition is a fixed side-view orange-and-cream commuter carriage, two large blue-dusk windows, low suburban roofs and utility poles outside, Conductor seated at the left bench, cream door machine near centre, narrow train door to the right, dark steel floor and underfloor silhouette.

### T01-01 · Borrowed Grid rebuilds the train · 0:00–0:04

Exterior side-tracking view. A train moves left to right through a rain-wet suspended power district. Borrowed Grid takes ownership of its body: enamel panels separate into a dark charcoal open frame; oxidized copper conduits run along the ribs; cyan current closes real circuits across the roof and wheel assemblies. The train is not orange-and-cream here.

Sound: rain on metal, exposed relay chatter, one rising current tone.

### T01-02 · Echo City rebuilds the train · 0:04–0:08

The same track line continues through a physical boundary into Echo City. The open electrical frame closes into a rounded old civic tram body: tarnished steel, oxidized brass, closely spaced gridded windows and warm stone-grey watercolor surfaces. A clock strike repeats in the wheel rhythm one beat late. The overall carriage length and travel direction remain continuous, but the material, roof and door framing are different.

Sound: current tone resolves into clock bell and damp wheel flange.

### T01-03 · Painted Country rebuilds the train · 0:08–0:12

The tram crosses a curling paper edge. Metal becomes warm off-white stock, graphite construction lines, dry-brush hatching and incomplete watercolor fills. Unpainted wheel guards and window frames exist only as pencil outlines until wet pigment runs from the front of the train and makes them physical. The train keeps moving; the world and the train share the same paper rule.

Sound: wheel rhythm becomes pencil scrape, paper flex and a wet brush pass.

### T01-04 · Museum releases Night Service · 0:12–0:18

The paper carriage enters Museum and is rebuilt again from ivory vellum, glass display-case panels, graphite archive marks and oxidized brass frames. The moving carriage then crosses one clean vertical world boundary. The Museum material remains on the left half while the right half resolves, from front to rear, into the worn orange-and-cream enamel Night Service exterior. Beyond the boundary is the exact blue-dusk suburban environment seen through Chapter 1's windows: low roofs, dark trees, utility poles and layered clouds.

The camera accelerates alongside the newly ordinary segment and aims at one large window. No portal explosion or digital glitch; the transformation is a physical change of surface and structure along the passing boundary.

### T01-05 · Through the glass, exact seamless handoff · 0:18–0:22

The camera passes through ordinary window glass into the now-normal carriage. The blue-dusk suburb remains continuous behind the glass; it does not dissolve into a second background. During 0:18–0:20.5 the H3 shot settles toward the exact Chapter 1 gameplay camera: two large dusk windows, Conductor at left, cream door machine near centre, narrow door at right and player at the authored start position. At 0:20.5, cross-dissolve to the exact real game-render plate `CH01_GAMEPLAY_FIRST_FRAME_REFERENCE_V01.png` and hold it for 45 frames. Do not regenerate, repaint or crop that final plate.

At video end, the game canvas starts on the identical full frame, including the existing `THE CONDUCTOR`, `DOOR` and `[E] SPEAK` prompts already present in the authoritative screenshot. Input and ambient motion unlock without any visual change at the boundary. No black frame, title card, camera jump, UI pop or background crossfade is allowed.

Handoff acceptance: overlaying the final video frame and the first gameplay frame at 50% opacity produces no visible double edge on the two window frames, door machine, narrow door, floor line or carriage shell.

## CIN 01→02 — CURRENT LEAVES THE TRAIN

Narrative job: turn the repaired Night Service chain into the electrical grammar of The Borrowed Grid; establish that the next city is inhabited and failing; leave a physical cyan lead ahead of Butch.

Duration: 22 seconds. Dialogue is voice-over; no lip sync.

### T12-01 · The last punch · 0:00–0:04

Close three-quarter shot of Butch's hands at the worn brass ticket press. He confirms the two endpoints with two fingers, punches the old ticket, and holds the lever down. The final Night Service machine settles rather than celebrating. Warm amber light appears inside the new ticket hole.

Sound: punch impact, pressure release, old carriage breathing.

### T12-02 · The train carries the answer · 0:04–0:08

Camera runs beside the amber pulse as it leaves the ticket, crosses an exposed brass pipe, passes the brake linkage and reaches the door relay. Every machine responds one beat after the pulse reaches it. The pulse exits the relay through a rain-wet copper service cable.

Caretaker, filtered: `One link restored.`

### T12-03 · Copper becomes city · 0:08–0:13

The camera follows the same cable through the window. Brass train pipe becomes a copper power line suspended hundreds of metres above a wet neon city. The carriage wall opens into a huge vertical industrial void; ladders, lift rails and dark evacuation blocks emerge from rain. Amber branches illuminate only infrastructure, not decorative neon.

Caretaker: `Five are still falling.`

### T12-04 · A line held elsewhere · 0:13–0:18

On a distant ceramic insulator, a real cyan fibre is tied around the copper line. Wind and rain pull it tight toward an evacuation tower outside the frame. Butch steps into the threshold and catches the wet fibre between two fingers. The thread pulls back with human resistance.

Sound: cable tension, distant residents, rain on enamel.

### T12-05 · Gameplay handoff · 0:18–0:22

Wide back view. Butch stands at the open carriage threshold facing the first ladder gap. The amber line leads to dead grid hardware; the cyan fibre continues beyond it. Hold the last composition for two seconds with usable clean motion: rain, cloth, hanging cable and one distant lift.

Handoff: Chapter 2 opening frame, no title inside generated footage.

Butch's hand crosses from rendered cloth into lightly drawn paper texture while his silhouette stays identical. Ahead, the next door exists only as a child's imperfect pencil outline. A thin cyan repair thread slides under it and catches on an unfinished latch line.

Butch stands before Mara's home mural. One player-chosen region has been washed to bare paper. The absence is cleanly visible and remains part of the composition. Pigment flows from the mural into the brush; the surrounding repaired car is not reset.

Butch paints the coupling-door construction line with the recovered pigment. The wet edge darkens, a hatched shadow appears, and the drawn door becomes physically load-bearing. He opens it; behind it is not another landscape but stacked vellum sheets held in glass.

## CIN 05→06 — THE MUSEUM CANNOT HOLD ONE WORLD

Narrative job: cash out the collected chapter relationships; make the museum's collapse an active forced alignment rather than an explosion; land directly on the moving train exterior and Chapter 6 fusion grammar.

Duration: 26 seconds.

### T56-01 · Four incompatible artifacts · 0:00–0:05

Butch places the last recovered artifact into the archive corridor. Four distinct objects now occupy their niches: a Looking Fragment, a copper bypass coil, a worn municipal service-key ring and a pigment-stained paper remnant. Amber paths connect each object to a different living diorama.

Sound: four material sounds answer in sequence: glass, copper, keys, paper.

### T56-02 · One row enforced · 0:05–0:10

The Archivist lowers a pristine white alignment grid. It slides the four cases toward one central axis and compresses their dioramas to identical dimensions. Butch braces both hands against two cases; the cyan thread crosses all four and grows physically taut.

Archivist: `One answer can still be saved.`

### T56-03 · Contradiction becomes boundary · 0:10–0:15

Glass does not explode. Hairline fractures follow the cyan thread and widen into controlled brush-shaped boundaries. On either side, different material laws remain active: neon copper grid, Echo City cobbles and clockwork, wet paper pigment, museum vellum, train enamel.

Mara, through the taut thread: `Then stop asking for one.`

### T56-04 · The floor starts moving · 0:15–0:21

The museum corridor tilts and lengthens. Ivory floor tiles become the roof ribs of the night train moving at speed. Vitrines become carriage windows containing the earlier worlds. Butch loses footing, catches the cyan thread and is pulled safely across the final dissolving glass edge.

Sound: museum HVAC becomes violent rail wind; all four material motifs enter the score.

### T56-05 · Gameplay handoff · 0:21–0:26

Wide three-quarter exterior on the moving train. Brush-shaped world boundaries travel along the cars without changing Butch's silhouette. Far ahead, Mara is only a small stable silhouette holding the other end of the cyan thread. A grid object, crowd rhythm, paper stroke and museum frame are visible as separate upcoming material zones. Hold two seconds with forward motion.

Handoff: Chapter 6 moving-train exterior / Fusion Spine opening.

## Editorial and sound assembly

- Use hard motivated match cuts inside each transition; reserve dissolves for pigment, steam and paper only.
- Each H3 source shot should be 5–6 seconds so the editor has handles. Final cut trims to the target shot times above.
- Add exact English subtitles after picture lock, within 8:5 safe area. Generated footage contains no text.
- Preserve two seconds of clean motion at every final shot for gameplay crossfade or last-frame replacement.
- Keep dialogue dry and close; avoid trailer narration. Environmental sound must carry the material change before music explains it.
- Music transition map: mechanical pulse → electrical polyrhythm → clock/tram rhythm → graphite scratch/paper breath → glass harmonics → full multi-material rail theme.

## Acceptance gates

1. A viewer can identify the destination chapter before its title appears.
2. Every transition begins with a real consequence from the chapter that just ended.
3. Chapter 3 contains no second Mara and ends with Butch boarding eastbound alone.
4. Chapter 4's blank scar survives the color bloom and museum conversion.
5. Chapter 5's collapse is forced alignment and material separation, not a generic explosion.
6. Butch's face, hair, jacket silhouette, age and proportions remain stable across all generated shots.
7. Cyan, amber and red retain their locked meanings.
8. The final two seconds of each film can be replaced by the actual next-chapter gameplay frame without rebuilding the preceding shots.
9. OPEN→01 ends on the literal Chapter 1 gameplay plate; an AI-generated lookalike is not acceptable as the final frame.
