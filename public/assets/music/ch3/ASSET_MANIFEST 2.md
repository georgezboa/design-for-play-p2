# Chapter 3 Music — Source and License Manifest

Last audited: 2026-08-12

## Release status

The underlying **compositions** in this folder are public domain in the United
States and in jurisdictions using the common life-plus-70-years term. That does
**not** establish that a particular performance or sound recording is free to
redistribute. Sound recordings have their own rights layer.

The files below arrived without performer, label, source URL, licence text, or
embedded attribution. Their hashes and technical details are recorded here so
that a future source recovery can identify the exact files. Until that recovery
is complete, they are approved only for the internal CS247G course build and
must **not** be included in a public or commercial release.

For a public release, replace every `recording source: unverified` entry with a
specific recording whose file page and licence permit game distribution, then
record the performer, uploader or label, licence, canonical URL, download date,
and new SHA-256. Wikimedia Commons is one suitable catalogue when the individual
file page supplies a compatible public-domain, CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA licence.
Follow the attribution and share-alike requirements of that individual file.

Authoritative licence guidance:

- [Wikimedia Commons: Licensing](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Licensing/en)
- [Wikimedia Commons: Sound recordings](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Sound_recordings)
- [IMSLP: Public Domain](https://imslp.org/wiki/IMSLP:Public_Domain)

## Required replacement record

When replacing a recording, preserve the runtime filename unless the corresponding
`C3_MUSIC` entry is updated. Add the following to this table before merging:

1. exact recording title and performer;
2. canonical file-page URL, not a search result or streaming page;
3. licence name/version and required credit text;
4. date retrieved and SHA-256 of the checked-in file; and
5. confirmation that the licence permits distribution inside a downloadable game.
