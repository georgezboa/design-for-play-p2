## Shared rules for all 3D props

- One isolated object or one inseparable functional kit per image/model.
- Neutral light warm-gray background, no environment, people, floor plane,
  dramatic shadow, text label, watermark or extra decoration.
- Three-quarter front view, entire silhouette visible with generous padding.
- Grounded 1960s–1980s European civic hardware: cast iron, oxidized brass,
  chipped cream enamel, dark hardwood, worn paper and dusty glass.
- Stylized realism with subtle painterly material variation. Keep construction
  mechanically plausible and readable at the fixed isometric camera distance.
- Real metre scale, centered pivot at ground contact, Y-up, no animation baked.
- Separate any part named as movable. No floating fragments, paper-thin load-
  bearing parts, tiny illegible ornament, baked sunlight or background geometry.
- Deliver GLB with embedded PBR materials. Preserve material slots for later
  recolor. Do not decimate before delivery; Codex will optimize and validate.
