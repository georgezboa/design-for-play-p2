# NIGHTFALL — Opening Animation Script V03

## Core idea

The train is not a permanent orange train and its windows do not contain separate worlds. It physically travels through archived worlds. Each world temporarily rebuilds the train in its own visual and material language. Only after the Museum releases it does the train become the ordinary orange-and-cream Night Service used in Chapter 1.

## Why the first image exists

The first image is a torn stack of mutually incompatible landscapes. It means that the failed world did not leave one reliable history; it left several conflicting versions. This establishes the problem before the Archivist or train appears.

## English voice-over

When the world began to fail, it did not leave one history behind. It left many.

The Archivist was built to preserve them: places, people, and choices that could no longer survive.

But the records contradicted one another. And as power ran out, the Archivist began forcing them into a single answer.

One archive remained beyond its reach: an old night train, carrying the last world seeds offline.

Each world remakes the train as it passes.

The train does not remember names. It remembers what someone did.
