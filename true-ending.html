THE RECORD BENEATH THE RECORD

THE UNFILED ENDING

The stones do not open another carriage. They open the part of Butch’s memory the Conductor kept rewriting.

There was no missing sister waiting at the end of the line.

Mara was an illusion made by the Conductor: a passenger-shaped answer built from Butch’s loneliness.

Butch never had a sister. Every rescue was the train teaching him to chase the absence it needed.
